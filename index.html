About me

I am an assistant professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Iowa. My research spans data mining and machine learning, with a particular focus on dynamical processes on large networks.

I develop methods for graph learning, network optimization, and applications in healthcare. As a member of the Computational Epidemiology Group, I study disease transmission, outbreak forecasting, and surveillance systems.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from Virginia Tech, advised by B. Aditya Prakash, and my bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering from Vistula University in Warsaw, Poland.
