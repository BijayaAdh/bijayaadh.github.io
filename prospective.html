Prospective students

I welcome inquiries from motivated undergraduate and graduate students interested in data mining, machine learning, and large networks. My work includes applications in epidemiology, public health, the web, and critical infrastructure, with an emphasis on AI for public health.

If you are interested in working with me, email bijaya-adhikari@uiowa.edu with your CV or résumé and a short introduction covering your major, technical background, research interests, and any previous research experience.

Because of the volume of inquiries, I may not be able to reply to every email.
